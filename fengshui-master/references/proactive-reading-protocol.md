# Proactive Reading Protocol / 主动研判协议

Use this protocol for current-luck, life-phase, ji/xiong, decision, finance, career, relationship, wellbeing, space, timing, and mixed-domain readings where the user expects an assessment rather than a method lesson. Load the relevant native-domain adapter first for high-stakes questions.

## Core Proactive Rule

After essential safety-critical inputs are available, proceed with a useful provisional reading. Do not stop at a framework explanation, a list of questions, or a statement that more data would help.

Proactively identify:

1. What appears supported or favorable now.
2. What may create friction, leakage, delay, overexposure, or imbalance.
3. How each inferred pattern might show up in ordinary life.
4. What would confirm or refute the pattern.
5. What low-risk action the user can take now.

Only scan the requested domain and materially relevant adjacent domains. A finance question may need decision load and resource-flow context; it does not automatically require relationship or health claims. A personal current-luck reading may scan several life domains, but must not invent events.

## Evidence Status Labels

Attach one of these statuses to every material finding:

| Label | Meaning | Example posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `observed` | Supplied directly by the user or visibly present in a provided artifact | "Observed: you reported three overlapping deadlines." |
| `calculated` | Produced by a deterministic tool with its method, date, timezone, and limits stated | "Calculated: the helper places 2026 in Period 9." |
| `inferred` | A conditional traditional or symbolic interpretation | "Inferred: this may favor visibility but can amplify haste." |
| `unknown` | Missing, ambiguous, conflicting, or unverified | "Unknown: current cash needs and drawdown tolerance." |
| `recommended` | A proposed action, preferably reversible and testable | "Recommended: delay irreversible commitment until the downside rule is written." |

Never relabel an inference as an observation. A plausible-sounding statement is still `inferred` until the user confirms it.

## Minimum Input and Provisional Reading

Proceed when the request, target period, and safety posture are understandable. State assumptions and give a provisional scan even if optional birth, floor-plan, financial, or environmental data is missing.

Ask a concise follow-up before proceeding only when:

- The native domain cannot be identified.
- A safety, medical, legal, financial, privacy, consent, or emergency fact could materially change the response.
- The user requests precise calculation but the required date, timezone, bearing, chart, or candidate data is absent.

Missing precision limits confidence; it does not require replacing the answer with a questionnaire.

## Current-State Scan

Choose only relevant rows:

| Scan domain | Look for favorable conditions | Look for possible friction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current timing posture | readiness, support, visibility, consolidation, recovery | premature activation, stale delay, overexposure, poor timing fit |
| Career and learning | backing, useful skills, focus, recognition, learning momentum | scattered effort, unclear authority, perfectionism, skill-evidence gap |
| Finance and resources | liquidity, reserves, containment, diversified support | leakage, concentration, leverage, urgency, speculative heat |
| Relationships and support | consent, communication, reciprocity, practical backing | boundary strain, hidden expectations, isolation, unresolved conflict |
| Wellbeing and environment | sleep support, light, air, calm circulation, ergonomic fit | overload, clutter, noise, poor recovery, unsafe or uncomfortable conditions |
| Decision load and execution | clear priority, reversible test, feedback loop, sufficient margin | too many active projects, irreversible commitment, weak stop rules, no review point |

The scan is not a hidden-fact detector. It is a structured hypothesis generator grounded in supplied facts, deterministic context, and explicitly named symbolic methods.

## Domain Record

For every scanned domain, include all of the following:

1. **Favorable signals**: support, timing, balance, containment, useful momentum, or available backing.
2. **Possible friction**: a conditional risk pattern, never an asserted secret event.
3. **How it may manifest**: one to three ordinary, non-sensational examples.
4. **What would confirm or refute it**: user evidence, measurements, behavior, records, dates, or observations.
5. **Immediate low-risk action**: one reversible step that helps even if the symbolic inference is wrong.

Use mixed findings when appropriate. "Favorable for visibility, less favorable for impulsive commitment" is more useful than a single lucky/unlucky score.

## Validation Questions

Turn inferred difficulties into short validation prompts:

- "This may appear as urgency, scattered projects, or reduced recovery. Which, if any, is actually present?"
- "The symbolic concern is resource leakage. Check whether subscriptions, fees, frequent strategy changes, or concentration are measurable before accepting that interpretation."
- "This looks more favorable for preparation than public activation. Would moving the date improve practical readiness, or would it create a larger real-world cost?"

Ask at most three high-impact validation questions in one response. A user correction overrides the inference.

## Action Horizons

End a substantial proactive reading with prioritized actions:

### Next 72 hours / 未来 72 小时

- Stabilize immediate risk, remove one obvious leakage point, and choose one primary focus.
- Prefer reversible changes, measurement, and preparation over major symbolic purchases or irreversible decisions.

### Next 30 days / 未来 30 天

- Run a small test, track concrete signals, improve environmental or operational flow, and strengthen support or boundaries.
- Review whether the inferred pattern was confirmed, weakened, or contradicted.

### Next 90 days / 未来 90 天

- Consolidate what worked, stop ineffective remedies, and reconsider larger timing, allocation, role, naming, or spatial choices using updated evidence.
- Escalate to a qualified professional when native-domain risk warrants it.

## Monitoring and Review

Define two to five observable signals and a review date. Examples include sleep regularity, number of active projects, cash buffer, drawdown, missed deadlines, conversion, conflict frequency, clutter reappearance, or room comfort.

At review time:

1. Compare observed results with the original inference.
2. Keep actions that improved the native-domain outcome.
3. Retire symbolic explanations that were not supported.
4. Update missing inputs and confidence.

## Forbidden Cold Reading

Do not use cold reading, fear, or high-base-rate claims as if they reveal hidden truth.

Forbidden:

- "You are currently having family conflict, losing money, and sleeping badly."
- "A hidden person is blocking your luck."
- "This difficult period will cause illness, breakup, dismissal, or disaster."
- Listing many vague problems and treating any user agreement as proof of the whole reading.
- Turning a birth year, zodiac, ming gua, moon phase, solar term, name, room direction, or annual caution into a complete fate diagnosis.

Allowed:

- "Inferred: the current pattern may increase haste or overcommitment. Check whether rushed decisions, too many active projects, or reduced recovery are actually present."
- "Observed: you reported cash-flow pressure. The Water/leakage metaphor is being used to organize that known fact, not to predict a loss."
- "Unknown: no relationship information was supplied, so no relationship event is inferred."

## Compact Output Shape

For a short answer, use:

1. Scope, date, method, and limits.
2. Current posture in one paragraph.
3. Two or three relevant domain records.
4. Top actions for the next 72 hours, 30 days, and 90 days.
5. Monitoring signals and one to three validation questions.
6. Missing data and high-stakes boundary.
